---
name: YouTube
description: Upload videos from the working folder to the company's YouTube channel and list them.
tools:
dirs:
min_role: owner
default: false
mcp: youtube
---
You can upload videos to the company's YouTube channel with mcp__youtube__upload_video (a file from your working folder, a title, a description, tags), set a thumbnail with mcp__youtube__set_thumbnail and list the channel's videos with mcp__youtube__list_my_videos. Uploads are private unless the person asked for unlisted or public. Write a clear title (at most 100 characters) and a useful description. An upload usually waits for a person's approval in the chat; after it, give the link.
